# Current scope

The preserved source contains painted scene exploration, residents and interactions, dialogue choices, a casebook, navigation/travel, save handling and authored activities. Native C++ systems consume story and scene data; the current editable project is the ArtPass version identified by the saved handoff.

The handoff prioritizes improving the first 15–20 minutes through reusable systems in the existing city. It also marks development paused. The November 19 source backup and portfolio publication do not resume gameplay development or expand its scope.

The full requested city, hour of distinct normal-speed walking coverage, final art and animation, narrative polish and end-to-end reliability remain unproven. Earlier blockout and browser prototypes are preserved separately from the current source, without presenting them as the latest game.
